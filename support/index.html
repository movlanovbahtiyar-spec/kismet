About Kısmet

Kısmet is a dating app exclusively for university students in Türkiye. Everyone signs up with their university email address (.edu.tr), so every person you meet is a verified student. Kısmet is for adults aged 18 and over. The app is available in Turkish, English, Russian and Arabic: change the language in Profile → Settings → Language.

Frequently asked questions

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Settings → Delete account. Your account, profile, photos, matches and messages are permanently deleted. Deleting your account does not cancel a Kısmet Plus or Premium subscription; cancel it separately in the App Store.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions → Kısmet. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What do Kısmet Plus and Premium include?

Kısmet is free with 30 swipes a day. Kısmet Plus gives you 70 swipes a day, lets you see who liked you, 3 sparks and 25 undos a day, and advanced filters. Kısmet Premium adds unlimited swipes, 10 sparks a day and unlimited undos. Both are available as weekly or monthly subscriptions. Full details are in the Terms of Use.

My purchase is not showing

Go to Profile → Memberships → Restore purchases. If the problem continues, email us.

How do I report or block someone?

Open the menu at the top right of their profile or chat and choose Report, Block or Unmatch. Reports are confidential: the other person never sees who reported them. Our team reviews every report. If you are in immediate danger, call 112 first.

I am not receiving the sign-in code

Check your spam folder; some university email systems delay external messages. You can request a new code after 90 seconds.

Why was my photo not accepted?

Profile photos are checked automatically against our Community Guidelines before they are published, and at least one photo must clearly show your face. If you think the decision is wrong, tap Appeal in the app and our team will review it.

How is my data protected?

We never sell your data or use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps. Your approximate location is rounded to about 1 km and never shown to anyone. Read the full Privacy Policy (in Turkish). For data protection requests under KVKK or GDPR, email [E-posta eklenecek].
